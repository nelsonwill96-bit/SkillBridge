Welcome to SkillBridge

SkillBridge is a web-based application designed to help organizations identify gaps in employee knowledge and prepare for employee absences.

When only one person knows how to complete a task, the organization may have difficulty continuing its operations if that person is unavailable. SkillBridge identifies these responsibilities and helps supervisors determine where cross-training may be needed.

What can SkillBridge do?

  • Track employees and their qualifications.
  • Identify responsibilities with limited coverage.
  • Simulate employee absences.
  • Recommend tasks for cross-training.
