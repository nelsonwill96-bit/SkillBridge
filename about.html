Why I chose this project

Working in a team environment has shown me the importance of sharing knowledge and making sure responsibilities do not depend on just one person. Even when an organization has enough employees, it may not have enough people trained to complete certain tasks.

I chose SkillBridge for my Capstone Project because it addresses a practical problem while allowing me to apply what I have learned about programming, web development, and data organization.
