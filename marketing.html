Why Use SkillBridge?

SkillBridge helps small organizations understand which responsibilities are most vulnerable when employees are unavailable.

Benefits

  • Identify single points of failure before they cause disruptions.
  • Help supervisors prioritize cross-training.
  • Improve knowledge sharing and continuity.
  • Use a simple web interface without specialized equipment.

Who could use SkillBridge?

Small businesses, IT departments, training coordinators, and other teams with specialized responsibilities could benefit from the application.

Proposed Pricing

The prototype is free to demonstrate. A future commercial version could be offered at an estimated $39 per month per organization. This is a proposed price, not a current subscription offer.
